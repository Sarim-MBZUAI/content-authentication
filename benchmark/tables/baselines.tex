\paragraph{Baselines.}
We compare against twenty zero-shot post-hoc detectors released between 2023 and 2026:
UFD~\cite{ufd},
FreqNet~\cite{freqnet}, NPR~\cite{npr}, FatFormer~\cite{fatformer}, AEROBLADE~\cite{aeroblade},
C2P-CLIP~\cite{c2pclip}, D3~\cite{d3}, FIRE~\cite{fire}, DDA~\cite{dda}, FerretNet~\cite{ferretnet}, WaRPAD~\cite{warpad},
AllPatchesMatter~\cite{allpatchesmatter}, OmniAID~\cite{omniaid}, PGC~\cite{pgc}, PROBE~\cite{probe}, DEAR~\cite{dear}, DGS-Net~\cite{dgsnet}, SICA~\cite{sica}, IAPL~\cite{iapl}, and ForensicConcept~\cite{forensicconcept}.
All baselines are evaluated without fine-tuning on our data.
